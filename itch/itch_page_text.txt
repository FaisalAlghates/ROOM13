# ROOM 13

*"There is no Room 13 on this level."*

**3:17 AM.** You wake on the floor of a hospital that has twelve rooms... and a thirteenth door that should not exist.

Something tall and silent walks these corridors. **He only moves when you don't look at him.** He cannot come near the light. So you keep the light on him - and run.

## What you do
- Explore a decaying hospital in the dark with a flashlight that is slowly dying.
- Keep him in the beam, back away, then break line of sight and run.
- Hide in lockers with the light off, throw a pipe to pull him away.
- Find the keycard, read the patient files, and learn why Room 13 is yours.
- In the final chase, sprint and **slide** under low pipes (hold Shift + W, then press C or Ctrl).

## Features
- First-person psychological horror, about 7-15 minutes
- A stalker AI that reacts to light, noise and whether you are looking at him
- Everything - level, textures and sound - is generated by code at runtime (small download)
- Live synthesised audio: fear-breath, heartbeat, rusty iron doors, tension music
- Full **English / Arabic** support (press L in the menu)
- Always-visible route marker so you never get lost - only scared

## Controls
**WASD** move - **Mouse** look - **Shift** sprint - **C / Ctrl** crouch (slide while sprinting in the last stage)
**E** interact / hide - **F** flashlight - **R** change battery - **G** or **Right-click** throw
**Tab** phone / map - **H** hint - **Esc** pause - **F11** window / fullscreen

## Install
1. Download and unzip `ROOM13_Windows.zip`.
2. Run **ROOM13_Launcher.exe** (Play Fullscreen / Play Window / Watch Auto-Play) or `Game\ROOM13.exe`.
3. Windows 10/11 64-bit, DirectX 12 GPU. **Headphones recommended. Play in the dark.**

*The exe is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: More info > Run anyway.*
